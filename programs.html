Program model

Think, build, launch, then keep going.

Find the sharp version of the problem.

  • Observe the user and context before choosing a solution.
  • Ask questions that reduce vague ideas into testable needs.
  • Challenge assumptions early, when changing direction is still cheap.

Make the idea visible and testable.

  • Prototype quickly with mentors nearby.
  • Use workshops to unlock specific skills at the moment they matter.
  • Change the solution when evidence demands it.

Show what works and what comes next.

  • Demonstrate the prototype rather than only pitching the idea.
  • Explain what was learned, where it failed, and what should happen next.
  • Judge clarity, insight, iteration, feasibility, and communication.

Build the infrastructure after the event.

  • Keep students connected to peers, mentors, parts, venues, and next challenges.
  • Grow technical communities around hardware, robotics, science, and software.
  • Use each program as a node in a larger builder network.
