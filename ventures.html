Program model

Think, build, launch, then keep going.

Find the sharp version of the problem.

  • Observe the user and real context before choosing a technical solution.
  • Ask questions that reduce vague ideas into crisp, testable needs.
  • Challenge assumptions early, when pivoting direction is still fast and inexpensive.

Make the idea visible and testable.

  • Prototype quickly with experienced hardware and software mentors nearby.
  • Use micro-workshops to unlock specific technical skills at the moment they matter.
  • Iterate the design rapidly when physical evidence demands it.

Show what works and what comes next.

  • Demonstrate working prototypes and demos rather than theoretical slide decks.
  • Explain what was learned, where failure occurred, and the roadmap forward.
  • Evaluate clarity, technical depth, feasibility, and presentation quality.

Build the infrastructure after the event.

  • Keep builders connected to peers, lab tools, mentors, components, and next-stage funding.
  • Grow permanent technical communities around robotics, embedded systems, and aerospace.
  • Use each program cohort as a node in the expanding Tachyon builder network.

Drones & Defense

Vector Aerotech

An advanced autonomous aerial systems and defense robotics startup originated within the Tachyon ecosystem. Vector Aerotech develops custom flight controllers, tactical reconnaissance UAVs, encrypted mesh datalinks, and heavy-payload multirotors. The venture has secured and executed mission-critical contracts across private enterprise infrastructure inspection, governmental geospatial surveillance, and defense/military aerospace applications.
