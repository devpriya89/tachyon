Our Vision

Care about what students leave with.

Depth over theater. Not just certificates or photos. A working prototype. A new technical skill. A clearer problem definition. A lifelong collaborator. A direct path forward into hardware, robotics, or game software.
The objective is continuity. A great Tachyon program should never feel like a one-day spike. It must build permanent momentum—connecting builders to mentors, parts, labs, and peers so they keep building long after the event ends.
Build with people who care.
