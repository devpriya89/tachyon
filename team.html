About Team

The crew behind the build.

Tachyon is structured like a small product studio for student experiences: sharp program thinking, technical mentors, campus partners, and on-ground operations working toward one outcome.

Care about what students leave with. Not just certificates or photos. A prototype. A new skill. A clearer problem. A collaborator. A next step.

How the team works

The team is designed around execution, not hierarchy. Every event needs someone to protect the learning arc, someone to support technical depth, someone to bring partners together, and someone to make the day run cleanly.

  • Program design: format, theme, judging, schedule, and learning path.
  • Technical mentorship: game development, AI, hardware, robotics, electronics, and astronomy support.
  • Partnerships: institutions, sponsors, education departments, maker networks, and community allies.
  • Operations: registration, venue flow, workshops, judging, media, and participant experience.

02Depth

04Ground

Event operators

Team principle

The objective is continuity.

A great Tachyon event should not feel like a one-day spike. It should make students more likely to keep building after the lights go off, the judging ends, and the room empties.
