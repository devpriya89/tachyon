About us

Platforms for young builders.

Tachyon works across student innovation, game development, hardware, robotics, astronomy, and experiential education. The formats change, but the philosophy stays direct: create the environment, tools, mentors, and urgency for students to build something real.

01 / Takumi

Craft. Code. Create.

A 24-hour offline game-development hackathon for under-18 builders in collaboration with DoE.

02 / AAROH

Think. Build. Launch.

A Delhi NCR student innovation hackathon focused on problems, evidence, prototypes, and iteration.

03 / Beyond Horizons

Learn. Look. Question.

Astronomy outreach that pairs theory with telescope interaction, observation, and open discussion.

The common thread

From a game overnight to a PCB on a workbench.

From a telescope in a school courtyard to robotics teams preparing for larger competitions, Tachyon is designed around action. Students leave with proof that curiosity can become an object, system, demo, or community.

Selected ventures

Events that turn intent into output.

Hackathons, meetups, outreach sessions, and developing robotics infrastructure, each tuned for students who want to make instead of only listen.
